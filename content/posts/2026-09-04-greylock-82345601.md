## We at Greylock are always searching for exceptional founders to help initiate and/or build the next iconic technology business with.

Today, we’re sharing a list of areas that we believe are ripe for innovation. If you are starting a company in any of these areas, we would love to chat with you.

While these are some areas we’ve spent time thinking about, we love when an entrepreneur introduces us to a thesis we’ve never imagined before. If you are thinking about starting a company, consider doing so withGreylock Edge, a bespoke 3-month company-building program designed to advance select pre-idea, pre-seed and seed founders from inception to product-market fit, with fully flexible financing.Apply hereor reach out directly to any Greylock investor for more info.

- AI-enabled utilities that transform broadly-applicable creative workflows
- AI-enabled commerce or travel discovery focused on faster, easier UX
- Social experiences that utilize games and/or AI to bring friends together

## Asheem Chandna

- Next generation IGA (identity governance and administration)
- AI-native data platforms

## Jerry Chen

- AI data engineer to make building data pipelines and ETL jobs easier
- AI powered business intelligence, both for horizontal use cases but also vertical or industry specific BI solutions

## Mike Duboe

- The modern marketing (SEO, advertising, measurement, identity) stack in the post-cookie, LLM-native world
- Vertical AI as a wedge to bring commerce in old-school verticals online
- Infrastructure to support cross-border ecommerce, in a world where trends are increasingly global and fast-moving
- Full-stack services firms that can operate with software-like margins due to AI

## Christine Kim

- Vertical AI agents automating professional services work in legal, financial services, accounting and tax
