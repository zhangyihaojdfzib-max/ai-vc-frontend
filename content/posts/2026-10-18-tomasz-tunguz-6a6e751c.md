In short :Anthropic is considered an AI company, but it should be valued as a cloud. On that basis, at $1.5t to $2t it would be worth roughly what Wall Street's estimates imply for a standalone GCP, on two-thirds the revenue.

We all consider Anthropic an AI company.

But what if it’s a cloud?

![Quarterly revenue for AWS, Azure, GCP and Anthropic from Q3 2024 through Q3 2026, with Anthropic rising from near zero to $16.8b per quarter](/images/posts/277813c47aa6.jpg)

Anthropic is growing much faster than the other clouds : 15x last year. GCP’s growth is far behind at 82%. But at current growth rates, Anthropic is closer to GCP’s pace, doubling next year.1

In revenue terms, Anthropic is about 30% the size of AWS & Azure & nearly half the size of GCP.

Anthropic is the fastest growing but the smallest of the four. At current growth rates, it would pass Azure in about three years & AWS in about three & a half.4Only GCP, now growing slightly faster than Anthropic’s current pace, would stay ahead. But Anthropic has the most concentrated business : 2 customers accounted for nearly 25% of revenue in 2025.5

Each started with a wedge to earn the cloud moniker : Amazon with ecommerce ; Google with search & ads ; Microsoft with the operating system. Anthropic’s angle : many of the best AI models in the world & the leading coding harness, Claude Code. 87% of businesses on Ramp that buy a coding agent buy Claude Code, up 56 points in a year.6

Anthropic exerts its pricing power in this market, charging more per task than others : Claude Opus 5.5 costs $5.98 per task on Artificial Analysis’ Intelligence Index, against $3.26 for OpenAI’s GPT-6 Astra & $1.99 for Google’s Gemini 4 Argon.7

It has the narrowest product offering, but it has made a record-breaking start with AI models, artifacts & design.

Growth needs capacity, & Anthropic has planned for it. It has made the largest data center commitments of the four : $518b, nearly 8x its $67b Q3 run rate, while AWS, Microsoft & Google have committed about 1x to 2x theirs.3

Each of these businesses is building its own chips, scaling data centers with debt, developing &/or reselling models, & managing infrastructure to support AI.

If it rains like a cloud & computes like a cloud… That’s how we should evaluate Anthropic.

Using Wall Street’s estimates, a standalone GCP would be worth roughly $1t to $2.3t.8At $1.5t to $2t, Anthropic would be valued equally to GCP, on two-thirds the revenue & far faster growth over the past year.9
