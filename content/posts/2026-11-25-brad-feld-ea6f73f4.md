One rescue at a time, a dedicated team rewrites the fate of abused and neglected farm animals.

The Great Rescue (working title) is a documentary Megan Sweeney of Big Heart Film is making about the people at Luvin Arms, an animal sanctuary in Erie, Colorado, and the abused and neglected farm animals they rescue. It follows two turkeys given a second chance at life: when they are rescued on Long Island, two women take them on a road trip to their new home at the sanctuary. There they meet a community of rescued farm animals, each with its own story, and the caregivers who look after them.

Megan, a Boulder filmmaker and photographer who has won four regional Emmys, is directing. Alex Lehmann (Paddleton,Blue Jay,Meet Cute) is the story producer, and Liz Marston of Skywalker Sound is doing the sound design. I’m an executive producer.
