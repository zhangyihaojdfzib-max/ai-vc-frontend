Private Inferenceconnects your own model endpoints toAI Gateway.

Enterprises running fine-tuned, self-hosted, or prerelease models used to need separate keys, client code, and logs for them. Those models can now run through the same authentication,SDKs and APIs, and observability ascatalog models, so every model your team uses is managed in one place.

Use Private Inference to:

- Switch between private and catalog models:Call private models through Chat Completions, Responses, or Messages. AI Gateway translates each request to your endpoint's format, so switching only changes the model ID.
- Keep models scoped to your team:Only your Vercel team can view or call private models, and they aren't listed in the public catalog. Credentials are stored encrypted, and you can enableZero Data Retentionper provider.
- Connect coding agents:Use private models in Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, and othercoding agentsconnected to AI Gateway.
- Monitor traffic together:View private model requests alongside catalog traffic inlogs,trace drains, andcustom reporting.Observabilitycharts break down requests, tokens, and time to first token by private model.
- Configure fallbacks:Optionallyfall back to a catalog modelif your private endpoint fails.

To get started, open thePrivate Inference pageand add your endpoint as a private provider. Enter its base URL, select its API format, and add its credentials. Then add the models it serves.
