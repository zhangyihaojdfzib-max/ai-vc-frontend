## The dynamics of distributed commerce have completely changed in just a handful of years.

In 2021, the App Tracking Transparency Act stripped commerce businesses of their primary method of finding and converting customers. Then the AI wave hit. Just as ATT closed the door on user-tracking and targeted ads, AI opened up new channels of engagement and personalization.

FERMÀTmakes it possible for commerce businesses to thrive in the post-ATT world. The company, which was founded shortly after the passage of ATT, provides the tooling and infrastructure to enable distributed commerce. While it didn’t start out as an AI company, FERMÀT quickly saw how the technology would supercharge its toolset.

“When you can’t track people anymore, the entire premise on which we interact on the consumer internet completely changes,” says Fermat CEO and co-founder Rishabh Jain. “At the same time, the rise of engagement with AI chat interfaces allows businesses to create a cohesive customer journey to support closed loop, one-to-one experiences. You need the transaction event to be directly correlated to the intention event.”

In other words, when companies can’t just use tracking tools to find and target customers with ads wherever they are, they need to get the most out of every interaction they have when the customers find them. Using Fermat, businesses can create a unique experience specific to each piece of content on the customer end – from clicking on the first ad that drew them into the business, to customization options for items they are viewing, to optimizing the final sale. AI makes it possible to deploy an essentially unlimited number of potential destinations and journeys, all the while measuring the efficacy of the increased conversion, transaction size, and stronger business.

“It’s a use case of AI that directly connects to the KPIs that the business cares about,” says Jain.

Jain spoke with Greylock partner Saam Motamedi on the Greymatter podcast about the double impact of ATT and AI on commerce and how Fermat is helping to build the next generation of online businesses. You can listen at the link above, or watch the video of this interview below.
