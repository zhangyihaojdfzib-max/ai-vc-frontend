You can now check your team’s billing overview, review and download invoices, and preview upcoming charges with theVercel CLI. All billing commands, including usage, contracts, and budgets, are now grouped undervercel billing.

Billing overview and invoice history are available on all plans, subject to your team’s existing billing permissions. Upcoming invoice estimates require an active paid subscription.

### Copy link to headingBilling overview

Check your team’s plan, billing status, billing cycle, balance due, and overdue invoices:

```
vercel billing overview
vercel billing overview --scope <team>
```

In CLI 65.17.0 and later, this overview also includes configured invoice details such as your billing address, tax ID, and purchase order.

### Copy link to headingInvoices and upcoming charges

Usevercel billing invoicesto review invoices, download PDFs, and preview upcoming charges. Forinspectanddownload, replace<INVOICE_ID>with a full invoice ID from thelistoutput:

```
vercel billing invoices list --limit 3
vercel billing invoices inspect <INVOICE_ID>
vercel billing invoices download <INVOICE_ID>
vercel billing invoices upcoming
```

Upcoming charges are estimates and may change until the end of the billing period.

For scripts and agents, add--jsontovercel billing overviewor anyvercel billing invoicessubcommand for structured output.

Existing usage, contract, and budget commands now also use the same prefix:

- vercel usage→vercel billing usage
- vercel contract→vercel billing contract
- vercel budgets→vercel billing budgets

vercel usage→vercel billing usage

vercel contract→vercel billing contract

vercel budgets→vercel billing budgets

The previous command names continue to work as aliases.

To get started,update your Vercel CLIwithvercel upgrade. Learn more in theBilling & Invoices documentation.
