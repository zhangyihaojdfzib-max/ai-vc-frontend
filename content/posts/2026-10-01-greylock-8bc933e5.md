My first 30-minute meeting with Carl turned into a two-hour walk through an empty 80,000 sq ft warehouse in Brisbane, California, talking about building America’s AI energy company.

There are certain people who make impossible missions feel inevitable. Carl is one of them.

He is charismatic, obsessive about details, and brimming with authenticity. We at Greylock are thrilled to co-lead the Series A in Parallax, the AI energy company.

A typical mechanical engineering student at Stanford builds dozens of components by graduation. An ambitious student, hundreds. Carl has built over 2,000. He drives a 1973 Camaro that he rebuilt himself. For his friend’s engagement, he designed a ring box with a superconductor chilled by liquid nitrogen that made the ring levitate in mid-air. Now he’s focused on solving the 100 GW, worth over $100bn, energy shortage facing America in the next 5 years.

Carl’s thesis is simple. The grid is sold out. The scaled solution for off-grid energy in the next 5 years is natural gas. But gas turbines, the most efficient way to convert natural gas to power, are also sold out until 2030+. Carl made the simple, yet profound observation that existing gas turbines are designed for a different customer: jet engines and large utilities. Those customers demand extreme reliability and efficiency. If an engine fails, hundreds of lives are at stake. Meeting those requirements takes feats of engineering and highly complex supply chains, all booked out for years.

But what do data centers need? Power, available now. Easy to install, and easy to produce at scale. By designing a gas turbine purpose built for the new customer in town, Parallax is able to use additive manufacturing (i.e. 3D printing) to control its own supply chain, and produce purpose built, scaled power. Parallax’s first product is a 10MW gas turbine, purpose built for data centers.

At Greylock, we’re primarily in the people business. When mission-driven entrepreneurs are tackling large markets, great things happen. We feel fortunate to partner with our co-investors Founders Fund, Eclipse, Lux, Diffusion and General Catalyst.

If you want to work on powering the next generation of AI, they’rehiring.
