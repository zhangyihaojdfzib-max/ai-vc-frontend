### The governance stack that earned the award

What sets Databricks apart in capital markets isn't just speed or scale. It's the governance architecture that makes AI trustworthy enough for regulated environments:

- Unity Catalogprovides a single control plane for access, lineage, and auditing across data and AI assets — so every query, every model output, every dashboard is traceable.
- Business semantics in Unity Catalog Semantics and Glossarylet firms define key measures — VaR, expected shortfall, liquidity buffers, exposure metrics — once and reuse them consistently across reports, dashboards, and AI applications.
- MLflowadds structured model governance: versioning, experiment tracking, evaluation, and controlled deployment into production.
- Comprehensive loggingcaptures who ran what queries or AI interactions on which data, supporting model risk management, internal audit, and supervisory reviews.

This is the difference between AI that demos well and AI that can withstand board-level and regulatory scrutiny.
