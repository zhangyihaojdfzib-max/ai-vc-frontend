You and your agents can now browse and search more than 1000 services available inVercel Connectwithout leaving your terminal.vercel connect discoversearches by name, alias, keyword, and description.

```
1vercel connect discover crm
```

Results can be filtered by category, connection method (such asmcporoauth) or the product a service exposes. You can also limit results to services that forward webhook triggers.vercel connect categorieslists the category slugs you can filter by. Both commands support--format=jsonfor scripts and agents.

```
1vercel connect discover --category ai --category data2vercel connect discover --connection-method mcp3vercel connect discover --supports-triggers
```

You can then pass the service name while creating any connector to the command:

```
1vercel connect create slack
```

Create a slack connector via CLI.

Update to version64.1.0 or laterof the CLI to get started, and read theVercel Connect CLI docsto learn more.
