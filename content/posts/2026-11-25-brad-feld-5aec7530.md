If you’re stuck at a gate this week with a delayed flight, or sitting in your kitchen waiting for your in-laws to land before Thanksgiving dinner, you can watch every plane flying near you onFlight Tracker. My brother Daniel built it.

It’s a live board of airliners, small planes, helicopters, and medical flights that updates itself every 30 seconds. Each plane gets a row showing what it is, where it’s going, how high and fast it’s flying, and which direction to look.

The board is a split-flap display, like the departure boards that used to clatter away in every airport and train station before they all got replaced by TV screens. Every time the board updates, the letters flip. Airline flights get a card on top with the airline’s logo and the route. A Switch View button in the corner gives you two other layouts.

You enter a US ZIP code, pick a radius between 5 and 20 miles, and leave it running on a laptop or any screen in your kitchen or office. When you hear a plane overhead, you glance over and see where it’s headed.

Daniel did this himself. He’s much better at UI than I am (he’s the one who found the themes I used forOverwatch), and he built the first version of Flight Tracker as a standalone app.

Daniel builds a bunch of apps, so we built a home for them calledIntensityLabsthat runs on ourIntensityMagicinfrastructure. Each of his apps gets its own web address, real sign-in, a waitlist, and a feedback button without Daniel having to build any of it, and he can ship changes to his own apps straight to production without going through our full release process. Flight Tracker was the first app we moved onto it.

Right now, Flight Tracker is an alpha. We’re finalizing the process for flipping a Labs app from alpha to production, which includes a thorough code review, security check, and complete move onto the IntensityMagic infrastructure. When that’s done, Flight Tracker will go through it.

In the meantime,request access. We’re letting people in from the waitlist a few times a day.

Happy Thanksgiving, and safe travels.
