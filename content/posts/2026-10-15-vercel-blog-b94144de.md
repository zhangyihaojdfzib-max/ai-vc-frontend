Vercel Token Pass is now in early access for Pro teams. For $30 per month, your team gets $80 in monthly credit for GLM 5.3 and Kimi K3, with more models to come.

Use Token Pass with coding agents such as fx, Command Code, and Pi, or build your own personal agent with the AI SDK. Token Pass runs throughAI Gateway, so you can choose your tools and switch between included models using your team’s shared credit.

Token Pass hasZero Data Retention (ZDR)enabled by default. Requests run through selected providers with inference in the US and EU. AI Gateway and the provider delete your prompts and responses after each request, and your prompts aren’t used to train models.

To get started:

1. Open theToken Passpage in your team’s AI Gateway dashboard.
2. Activate your subscription, create a Token Pass API key, and follow the setup instructions to connect your agent.
3. Select GLM 5.3 or Kimi K3, and start building.

The subscription renews monthly unless canceled. Unused credit doesn’t roll over. When your credit runs out, Token Pass requests stop until the next billing period, with no automatic overage charges.

Learn more in theToken Passdocumentation.
