Who will be the first $10t company? The one that first crosses the trillion dollar chasm : building a $1t AI compute engine before AI erodes the legacy cash cow paying for the GPUs.

In the last few weeks, two CEOs have put a $1t revenue target on their businesses : Andy Jassy & Elon Musk. We are tracking each company’s cloud & AI compute engine toward $1t in annualized segment revenue1.

Andy Jassy, President & CEO of Amazon2:

“We believe that AWS can grow north of 40% in each of 2027 & in 2028, & be a trillion-dollar revenue business in the next 5 to 10 years.”

Elon Musk, on SpaceX’s Q3 call3:

“SpaceX could reach a $500b to $1t annual recurring revenue run rate by 2027.”

![Google Cloud Growth Rate Is 2.1x Azure & 2.6x AWS](/images/posts/14a389e981b3.jpg)

Meanwhile, growth rates are accelerating at every hyperscaler on businesses generating $30b to $50b per quarter4.

![Google Cloud & SpaceX Cross $1t Annualized in 2030 ; AWS in 2032](/images/posts/bf43067d0c78.jpg)

Assume current growth rates with a 15% annual decay & Andy Jassy’s guided 40%+ growth in 2027 & 20285. Google Cloud overtakes Azure in mid-2027 & passes AWS by the end of 2027 to become the largest of the three cloud businesses6.

On that trajectory, Google Cloud & SpaceX both cross $250b in quarterly revenue ($1t annualized run rate) in 2030 & AWS follows in 2032 : provided power, silicon & balance sheets hold.

![Alphabet & Amazon Fall 12% to 13% on Earnings While Microsoft Holds Flat](/images/posts/28095aad623a.jpg)

Why did the market sell off two of the three hyperscalers on earnings day? Investors are worried that the legacy businesses will not be able to sustain the positive cash flows to continue the capex spend in sufficient time to realize the ROI7.

Philipp Schindler, SVP & Chief Business Officer of Google8:

“Look, in Q3, Search grew 12% year-over-year to $63b… it’s worth noting that the strong growth in AI Mode queries had an impact because of the lower monetization we were seeing in AI Mode.”

Alphabet’s search queries & visual searches are at record highs, with AI Mode queries running 3x the length of traditional searches, but Search & other advertising growth slowed to 12% ($63.3b), down from 19% in Q1 & 17% in Q2, as AI Mode monetizes at a lower rate than classic ten-blue-link search8.

Amazon’s retail & advertising machine faces the same question : whether external shopping agents bypass the search results pages where Amazon Ads generates $19.6b per quarter (up 12% in Q3, down from 26% in Q2).

Andy Jassy, President & CEO of Amazon2:

“The natural question is whether agentic shopping from external horizontal agents is meaningfully impacting Amazon Ads, & the answer is no. Few customers are doing much shopping this way yet, as the experience is still not great nor more convenient than coming to a retailer directly to research or one-click order there.”

Primarily Google & Amazon must cross a new chasm : refocusing capital & product around hardware, gigawatts & agentic compute while their historical cash cows absorb the shock of AI.

Microsoft is the only hyperscaler whose legacy enterprise franchise is expanding ARPU through AI faster than seat growth is slowing9. ServiceNow is seeing the opposite : its market cap fell 29% on fears of AI erosion of its core seat-based business10.

Google must prove that three-times-longer AI queries & autonomous shopping agents can monetize at the same yield as ten blue links. The first $10t company will be the one whose new compute engine reaches $1t before its old monopoly pays the tab.
