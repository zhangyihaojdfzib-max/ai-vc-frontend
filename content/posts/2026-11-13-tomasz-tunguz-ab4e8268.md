In short :AI agent traffic jumped 8x between August & October 2026 as bots reached 54% of global web traffic. Yet United Airlines & Amazon are blocking shopping bots at checkout because ads, ancillary upgrades, trip insurance & extended warranties represent 12% to 20% of revenue & 100% to 127% of operating profit.

Bots passed 51% of global web traffic in 2024 & reached 54% in 2026, while monthly AI agent traffic jumped 8x in two months between August & October 2026.1

![Bots now exceed 54% of web traffic as AI agent traffic surges 8x in two months](/images/posts/5cc887fe8741.jpg)

Instead of welcoming those agentic buyers, the largest consumer storefronts are locking them out.

This week United Airlines updated its terms to ban AI agents from booking tickets, reserving the right to cancel any reservation bought by a bot.2Amazon has similarly blocked third-party AI shopping agents from its store.3

Both companies cite security. The deeper reason sits on their income statements : in modern e-commerce, the base product merely breaks even ; ads, upgrades & warranties supply the profit.

Amazon collected $588b of retail revenue last year, but advertising is the only profit in it. The $68.6b of sponsored listings, at roughly a 50% margin, generate $34.3b : all of the $34.4b in retail operating income.4

United runs the same playbook with fees instead of ads. Its $4.8b of seat & bag upgrades, $3.2b of credit card & loyalty fees & trip insurance commissions together top its entire $4.7b of operating income.56

Dell’s computer business is a high-tech grocery store. Hardware sold at a 13.7% gross margin does not cover $14.6b of operating expenses ; ProSupport warranties & attached services at a 44.8% margin generate $10.4b of gross profit, or 127% of Dell’s $8.1b in operating income.7

Each share tops 100% because the base product, priced near cost, loses money on its own.

Shopify runs the opposite model, which is why it welcomes agents. Shopify powers independent merchant websites & collects a 3.1% toll on every dollar processed, 78% of it from payment processing & merchant services.8Whether a human or an AI agent clicks buy, Shopify collects the same cut. In September, it opened checkout to browser agents across every eligible store.

An AI agent is a buyer on a mission. It skips the sponsored listings, declines Economy Plus unless explicitly instructed & unchecks the extended warranty on a Dell Latitude.

Merchants built on loss leaders now face a choice : raise base prices, charge an explicit agent checkout fee, or invent wholesale incentives that persuade bots to upsell. Until they re-price the funnel, the sign on the checkout window stays the same : no bots allowed.
