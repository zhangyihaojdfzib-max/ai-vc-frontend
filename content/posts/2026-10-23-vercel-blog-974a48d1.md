### Copy link to headingView, set, and remove a budget

This is the same On-Demand Budget shown in your team's billing settings. Setting a budget does not stop usage on its own. The--pauseflag pauses production deployments of all projects when spend reaches 100%.

### Copy link to headingList budget alert history

You can now see your budget alert history. Runvercel budgets alertsfor the full log, filtered with--since,--until,--limit, and--json. A barevercel budgetsprints the three most recent alerts.

```
1$ vercel budgets alerts2> 3 budget alerts on team my-team3  date                    budget    threshold      spent    budget amount    actions configured4  2026-09-21 14:33 UTC    team            50%    $313.33             $600    notify5  2026-09-19 23:12 UTC    team           100%    $313.33             $250    notify, pause6  2026-09-19 02:35 UTC    team            75%    $230.00             $300    notify
```

Managing budgets requires theOwnerorBillingrole. Spend Management is available at no additional cost to Pro teams and to Enterprise teams on the Flexible Commitment plan.
