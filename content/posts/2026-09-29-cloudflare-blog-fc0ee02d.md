## Govern access and agent behavior

Agentic traffic operates in the space between automation and human: tasks delegated by people, executed by software. This changes how access decisions must be made. These questions can be hard to answer. A recognized agent with a long history of legitimate activity may have high trust, but an unusual action can still create immediate risk. Cloudflareâs approach is to keep trust and risk signals separate, thereby giving application owners more control than a single bot score or allow-or-block decision, and providing more powerful tools to quickly adapt to change.
