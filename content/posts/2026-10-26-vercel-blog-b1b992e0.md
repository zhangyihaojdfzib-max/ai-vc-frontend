Vercel Agentcan now investigate alerts fromVercel monitorsbefore they reach your paging system. With validation enabled for a webhook destination, only alerts Agent classifies asCriticalare sent to it.

To enable validation:

1. Open a monitor’s notification settings and create or link a webhook destination for your paging system, such as incident.io.
2. SelectCritical Onlynext to that destination.
