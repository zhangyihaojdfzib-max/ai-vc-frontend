Enterprise teams usingSecure Computecan now create and manageAWS PrivateLinkendpoints directly from the Vercel dashboard.

Endpoints on Secure Compute live in your team's dedicated VPC and use the network's AWS region. They belong to the network and are shared by its connected project environments.

Vercel Functions,Vercel Sandbox, and builds connected to the network can reach AWS-hosted backends over the AWS private network. Before you add an endpoint in Vercel, the backend must already be exposed through a published AWS PrivateLink endpoint service. Examples include RDS, Aurora, Redshift, Snowflake, MongoDB Atlas, and internal services behind a Network Load Balancer.

In Team Settings →Networking, open your Secure Compute network and chooseAdd Endpointfrom thePrivateLinktab.

Standard pricing for new endpoints:

- Each Secure Compute network includes one AWS PrivateLink endpoint. Additional endpoints cost $30 per month each.
- PrivateLink data transfer costs $0.04 per GB.
