## Copy link to headingApplications in any language with containers

TheVercel container runtimelets you deploy applications written in any language, packaged with the OS libraries and executables they need. Since container support entered Public Beta, 5,000 teams have shipped 150,000 container deployments on Vercel.

Containers deploy as Vercel Functions on Fluid compute, scaling with traffic and usingActive CPU pricing, which doesn’t charge for idle time spent waiting for API responses, database queries, or LLM inferencing. Vercel Services lets you develop and deploy your backend alongside your frontend, whileVercel CDNserves traffic on your domains,Vercel WAFprotects it, logs help you investigate issues, and rollbacks restore earlier deployments.

## Copy link to headingBackground workers with Vercel Daemons

Vercel Daemonslets you deploy containers as continuously running background workers on Fluid compute. You choose the number of instances in each region, and Vercel maintains that capacity by restarting processes when they exit or crash.

Daemons launches with support for pull-based workloads, where your code requests work from another system and processes it as it becomes available. That could mean consuming queue messages or fetching pending jobs from a database.
