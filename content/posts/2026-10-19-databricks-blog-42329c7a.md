## Profile Agents Private Preview: Build and use Customer360 profiles directly in Databricks

Profile Agents, entering Private Preview today, helps data teams unify scattered customer information from dozens of sources into trusted Customer 360 profiles directly in Databricks, giving marketers a consistent customer view for better segmentation and personalization.

Its capabilities address three connected tasks:

- Source normalization:Field mapping and semantic normalization bring source data from different sources into a consistent structure and meaning for downstream use.
- Agentic Identity Resolution:Deterministic, probabilistic, and LLM-based matching help connect fragmented records. Golden record survivorship determines which attributes to retain in the unified profile.
- Stateful, incremental processing:Micro-batch updates keep profiles current as data changes, while manual data steward overrides allow teams to apply human judgment where needed.

Profile Agents and Campaign Agents can be adopted independently. Teams can start with campaign workflows using their existing customer data or use Profile Agents to improve how they prepare and unify customer records.
