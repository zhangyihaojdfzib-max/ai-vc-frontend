### Set a baseline sampling rate

You can enable tracing on any domain andset a baseline sampling rateÂ to balance visibility, data volume, and cost. You might trace 1% of requests during normal operation, giving you a continuous view of request behavior without collecting a trace for every request.

### Configure Trace Rules

Trace RulesÂ let you keep a low baseline sampling rate while capturing complete traces for a specific investigation. If one customer reports a problem, you can trace 100% of traffic for their hostname, source IP, or identifying request header while leaving everyone else at 1%. Or during an investigation, you could trace 100% of requests carrying a temporary debug header, while leaving all other traffic at the baseline. This lets you reproduce an issue without increasing tracing across the entire domain.

Trace Rules use the sameCloudflare Rules language, so you can target paths, methods, headers, IP addresses, geographies, or combinations of those properties.
