## The relationship between humans and AI is not a competition, but a partnership.

At least, that’s what an AI-powered chatbot told me in our most recent conversation for Fireside Chatbots, a special podcast miniseries running as part of Greymatter’sIntelligent Futureseries on artificial intelligence. For this podcast, I don’t just talk about AI – I talkwithAI. My guest is ChatGPT, a large language model created byOpenAI, an organization whose Board of Directors I serve on.

This week, ChatGPT and I dive into the specific ways AI can amplify or augment humans (rather than replacing them). Taking things a step further thanour previous conversation, this time I experimented with different styles of phrasing and prompts in an effort to push ChatGPT out of its default mode.

Obvious examples include handling repetitive tasks, which allows humans to focus on more creative and complex endeavors, or extending our data analysis capabilities to the point where we can conduct scientific research faster, develop personalized educational programs, or increase sales. Additionally, ChatGPT and I discussed the huge advantage AI could bring both to high achievers and to those looking to level up their abilities.

We also delved into the more recent revelations about the potential for AI with the advent of artistic tools like DALL-E, which I believe will be a boon for creativity on a major scale. As both ChatGPT and I suggested, this will undoubtedly raise the bar for technical skill and efficiency, in the process raising the value of truly unique ideas and innovation.

“As AI technology continues to advance, access to tools like DALL-E will become more widespread and the ability to create and produce will become more democratized…” ChatGPT told me. “..this is where human creativity and ingenuity will be crucial in complementing the technical capabilities provided by AI tools.
