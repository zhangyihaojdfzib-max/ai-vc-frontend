Today we’re launchingVercel X, a six-week accelerator for AI founders at the idea and pre-seed stages.

Join a cohort of 40 teams at our San Francisco HQ and build alongside Vercel’s product leaders, designers, and engineers.

The program takes no equity for participation and offers access to a program-wide pool of more than $9M in credits from Vercel, AWS, Anthropic, OpenAI, and other partners.

Applications are now open through November 29, 2026. The program runs from January 19 to March 2, 2027.

Vercel has worked with more than 100 early-stage teams from around the world across three accelerator cohorts. Alumni includeRex, Alloy Robotics,Interfaze,Cervo, andHacktron. Interfaze and Rex are backed by Y Combinator, and Cervo was acquired by Altana Technologies.

## Copy link to headingWhy Vercel X?

If you’re applying to Vercel X, you probably have an idea or project you haven’t been able to stop thinking about. The next stretch is figuring out exactly what you’re building and who it’s for.

We think you get through that stretch the Vercel way: by deeply understanding your customers, shipping quickly, and iterating to greatness.

The program includes:

- Technical mentorship:Hands-on workshops and product feedback from Vercel’s design, product, and engineering teams.
- Partner credits:Put credits toward hosting, compute, and model API usage as you build and test your product.
- Founder community:A cohort of AI founders at the same stage, with weekly fireside chats featuring industry leaders.
- Investor access:Mentorship from VC partners and the opportunity to present your company to investors, ecosystem partners, and the Vercel community at Demo Day.

## Copy link to headingWho should apply

We’re looking for technical builders with ambitious ideas and insatiable curiosity.

You don’t need a finished product or traction. Tell us what you want to build, who needs it, what AI makes possible, and why you’re the right person or team for the job.

Solo founders and teams of up to three can apply from around the world. You must be a Vercel customer and must not have participated in a traditional VC accelerator or raised an institutional seed round. See the additional eligibility requirements below.

All participants must spend the full six weeks in San Francisco and attend weekly events in person.
