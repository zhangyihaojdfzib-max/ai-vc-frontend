## FAQ for instructors

### How do I distribute assignments?

Distribute through your existing LMS or a GitHub repository. Export the assignment notebook as an.ipynbfile and share it with any datasets or instructions; students import a copy into their Free Edition workspace, complete it, and export the finished notebook for submission. For group assignments, students can share a notebook and collaborate in real time.

### How do I distribute large datasets to students?

Three options for datasets too large for GitHub or a local download: (1) host the dataset in cloud storage students connect to; (2) useOpen Sharingto share the dataset from your Free Edition workspace directly into each student's workspace; or (3) pick a free dataset fromDatabricks Marketplacethat students access natively inside the platform.

### Can I use Databricks for group assignments?

Yes. Databricks is built for collaboration — with real-time co-authoring, comments, and tagging, students can work together and invite you into their notebooks to troubleshoot or give feedback. Databricks Free Edition is designed for up to five people to share an account for a group project. Seehow to set up collaboration in Databricks Free Edition.

### What if students run out of compute during an assignment?

Free Edition runs on smaller compute sizes under fair-use limits. If a workspace exceeds its quota, its compute pauses for the rest of the day and resumes the next day. Hitting a limit mid-assignment is rare and never permanent. Furthermore, you and your students can double your available daily credit up to three times per year, so students can always keep working during crunch periods and exams. The best way to ensure a smooth experience is to test each assignment in Free Edition ahead of time to catch any step that needs significant compute.

### Is Free Edition really free, and is it right for a course?

Yes - it's perpetually free, with no cloud contract and no credit card required to sign up. It's built for learning and explicitly not for production or commercial use, which fits a classroom well. Credits reset daily, so students start each day fresh.
