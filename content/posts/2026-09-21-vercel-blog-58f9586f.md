Deployments now show their billable duration and CPU minutes in the dashboard,vc inspect, and theREST API. Use it to understand how each build contributes to your usage.

![](/images/posts/5934713b1ad3.jpg)

![](/images/posts/86ba1923b8e7.jpg)

Billable duration is the build and post-build time combined, rounded up to the next whole minute. CPU minutes are that figure multiplied by the machine's vCPUs.

To see the same breakdown from the CLI, update to version 59.23.1 or later withnpm i -g vercel@latest, then runvc inspect:

```
1vc inspect <deployment>2
3  Duration4
5    build duration       5m 9s6    post-build duration  3m 21s7    billable duration    9m8    CPU Minutes Usage    270 minutes (9m x 30vCPU)
```

Learn more aboutbuild machines and CPU minutesin the docs.
