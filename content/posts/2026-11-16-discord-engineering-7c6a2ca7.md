# Give Discord a Vice City Makeover

It's just under 72 hours until we get to return to Vice City on November 19. If you’re like us and can’t wait to get a little Grand Theft Auto VI vibe going early, we’ve got just the thing.

Starting today, you can apply theVice City color theme, app icon, and Display Name Style.

## How to Apply the Vice City Theme

The limited-time Vice City theme is available on both desktop and mobile, so you can match your look with Vice City wherever you are. It’s available to everyone through December 13 and to Nitro members (excluding Nitro Basic) through February 20. ​

During the first week, from November 16–22, you can tap the button below to navigate straight to the theme in the Quest Home and apply it immediately.​

![A screenshot of Discord’s desktop UI with the Vice City color theme applied. ](/images/posts/cab7473a8d9d.png)

After November 22, you’ll find the Vice City color theme and app icon in eitherUser Settings → Appearance on desktop, or Settings → App Settings on mobile. The Display Name Style will be available the next time you go to customize your profile. ​
