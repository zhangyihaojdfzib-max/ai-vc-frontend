Vercel Blob can now delete blobs automatically. PassttlDayswhen you write a blob and it is deleted that many days after upload, from 1 to 730 days. The TTL counts from the blob's upload date.

Use TTLs for blobs you would otherwise delete yourself, such as session transcripts, exports, and temporary uploads.

ttlDaysworks onput(),copy(),putImage(), multipart uploads, and presigned upload URLs. Every write result andhead()response now includesexpiresAt, so you know when a blob is due for deletion.

```
1import { put, setTtl } from '@vercel/blob';2
34const blob = await put('exports/report.csv', csv, {5  access: 'private',6  ttlDays: 7,7});8
9console.log(blob.expiresAt); 10
1112await setTtl(blob.url, { ttlDays: 30 });13await setTtl(blob.url, { ttlDays: null });
```

setTtl()changes or removes the TTL of blobs you already have, one URL or up to 1,000 at a time, without re-uploading them.

InVercel CLI 65.16.0and later, pass--ttl-daystovercel blob put,put-image,copyorpresign, and usevercel blob set-ttlon existing blobs.

```
1vercel blob put report.csv --ttl-days 72vercel blob set-ttl exports/a.csv exports/b.csv --ttl-days 303vercel blob set-ttl exports/a.csv --clear-ttl
```

Setting a TTL costs oneAdvanced Operationper started 60 days, on top of the write. A 7-day TTL costs one, and the 730-day maximum costs 13. Clearing a TTL and the deletions themselves are free. You can track TTL operations in the Blob section of the Observability tab.
