# Paul Christiano joins OpenAI Foundation Board

Paul Christiano will also join the Foundation’s Safety and Security Committee.

We’re announcing the appointment of Paul Christiano to the OpenAI Foundation Board. He will be a non-voting observer on the OpenAI Group PBC Board.

Paul will also join the Safety and Security Committee (SSC) of the Foundation Board, working alongside its chair, Zico Kolter. The SSC provides governance over safety and security practices across all of OpenAI, including OpenAI Group PBC.

Paul brings government experience spanning two administrations through his work at theCenter for AI Standards and Innovation⁠(opens in a new window)(CAISI) within the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST), an agency of the U.S. Department of Commerce, where he is a Senior Tech Advisor1. During his time at CAISI and its predecessor, the U.S. AI Safety Institute, Paul has worked on evaluating frontier AI models, including capabilities with national security implications, and on approaches to mitigating associated safety and security risks.

Paul is also the founder of theAlignment Research Center⁠(opens in a new window)(ARC), a nonprofit research organization focused on aligning advanced AI systems with human interests. From 2017 to 2021, Paul led alignment research at OpenAI and contributed foundational work on reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF). In this new capacity, Paul brings a distinct and technically grounded perspective to OpenAI’s governance decisions. Our mission is to ensure that artificial general intelligence benefits all of humanity.

- Bret Taylor, Chair of the OpenAI Foundation and OpenAI Group PBC Boards
- Paul Christiano

AI is entering a new era of capability, making it increasingly important that advances in these systems are accompanied by strong safety, security, alignment and governance. Throughout Paul’s career researching AI alignment, he brings a perspective that takes seriously the possibility that advanced AI could pose catastrophic risks and that developers must prepare before systems reach more dangerous levels of capability. He has been an independent voice on whether the industry’s safeguards are adequate. We believe the Foundation’s governance is stronger with people prepared to challenge prevailing assumptions and help strengthen the evidence, safeguards, and accountability underlying critical decisions.

Paul’s appointment builds on thegovernance structureestablished through OpenAI’s recapitalization in October 2025, including the commitments made in connection with the reviews conducted over the course of a year by theCalifornia Attorney General⁠(opens in a new window)andDelaware Attorney General⁠(opens in a new window). Their engagement helped strengthen the governance framework.

## About the OpenAI Foundation

The OpenAI Foundation is a charitable organization dedicated to ensuring AI benefits all of humanity. As a separate nonprofit organization, the Foundation conducts its own charitable operations while maintaining control of, and holding asignificant equity stakein OpenAI Group PBC. The Foundation supports initiatives advancing scientific discovery, strengthening civil society, and building resilience through responsible AI. Through grants, partnerships, and other philanthropic initiatives, the Foundation helps accelerate work that creates lasting public benefit and expands access to AI’s transformative potential.

The Foundation controls OpenAI Group PBC, with the Safety and Security Committee a committee of the Foundation Board. Learn more atwww.openaifoundation.org⁠(opens in a new window).
