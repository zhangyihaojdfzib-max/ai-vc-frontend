## Enabling teams to develop, test and validate AI systems in high-fidelity simulation.

Today, we’re excited to announce that Greylock is leading the Series A inAntioch, a company building the simulation platform for physical AI.

AI has transformed how quickly software teams can build products. Physical AI is poised for a similar transformation. Advances in foundation models are expanding what robots, drones, and autonomous systems can do. Realizing that potential requires changing how these products are developed, tested, and deployed.

Physical testing remains the key bottleneck. Validating new capabilities, model updates, and hardware changes requires equipment, facilities, and engineering time. It slows development and limits what teams can test, especially rare failures that are difficult to reproduce. As models become more capable, the speed of validation will increasingly determine how quickly teams can bring products to market.

Antioch brings the speed of software development to physical AI. Its platform calibrates high-fidelity simulations to customers’ hardware using real-world data, then runs them at cloud scale. Teams can move from a single experiment to thousands of parallel evaluations, identify weaknesses, and validate improvements before deployment. The result is faster development, broader test coverage, and greater confidence in each release. Antioch is already working with leading physical AI teams, including Amazon’s Ring, which have accelerated the speed at which they can develop and bring products to market.

The Antioch founding team combines company-building experience with deep technical expertise. Harry Mellsop, Alex Langshur, and Michael Calvey previously built Transpose together, which was acquired by Chainalysis. Harry also worked on computer vision at Tesla, while Collin Schlager brings experience in simulation, machine learning, and hardware from Meta Reality Labs. Together, they understand the complexity of physical systems and the infrastructure engineers need to build them.

At Greylock, we believe the companies that turn advances in AI into reliable products will help define the next generation of industry. Antioch is building a core platform for that transition, enabling teams to learn faster and deploy with confidence.

We’re thrilled and privileged to back the Antioch team as they bring software speed to the physical world.
