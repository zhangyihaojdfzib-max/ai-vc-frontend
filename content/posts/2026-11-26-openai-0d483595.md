# More ways to move care forward with ChatGPT for Healthcare

Introducing computer-use agents, a new healthcare model configuration, and new healthcare plugins that work across healthcare software to get real work done.

Healthcare teams need to piece together information from the EHR, hospital policies, scheduling systems, and payer portals to deliver care and manage operations. ## Carry work forward across healthcare systems with computer-use agents

Organizations often need to move information between disconnected systems to prepare documentation, coordinate care, and complete administrative tasks. Computer-use agents can help with these steps by navigating the software that teams already use: reading screens, clicking, typing, and moving between applications.

Today, we’re sharing advances in computer-use agents for healthcare, including more capable, safe, and affordable models for clinical and administrative workflows, and access controls and audit logs for secure deployments. We’re also sharing our work with healthcare organizations that are deploying agents, including Abridge, Ambience, Cigna Healthcare, and CVS Health, to process referrals, move documents between systems, and bring together the information care teams need. ## Stronger clinical answers, everywhere teams work

We’re improving trusted clinical search in ChatGPT for Healthcare and ChatGPT for Clinicians, including how ChatGPT finds, evaluates, and cites medical evidence. These updates help clinicians connect relevant evidence to clinical questions and translate it into practical next steps, with citations they can inspect.

To measure the impact of these updates, in a blind evaluation, physicians reviewed responses to real-world clinical questions across multiple specialties, including multi-turn conversations. In over 1300 reviews, physicians rated the updated experience more highly across all eight dimensions evaluated, including factual correctness, safety, actionability, and source authoritativeness.

## Build trusted clinical search into your applications with Healthcare model configuration

Developers can now bring cited medical evidence into their applications with a healthcare configuration of select OpenAI models, including GPT‑6.2 Astra and GPT‑6.2 Sol. Available through the Responses API today, the configuration pairs the models with a harness tailored to clinical questions and trusted clinical search.

GPT‑6.2 Astra with the healthcare configuration ranked first among the systems we evaluated on HealthBench Professional, a benchmark of 525 challenging tasks written by physicians to test how well AI can answer questions about patient care, draft clinical documentation, and synthesize medical evidence. It earned a length-adjusted score of 74.4%, 6.3 percentage points ahead of the highest-scoring non-OpenAI system, Claude Fable 5.2 with web search.

All model configurations have web search enabled and are evaluated at their highest reasoning effort.

During a visit, clinicians rely on rapid point-of-care responses. On HealthBench Professional, GPT‑6.2 Astra configured for healthcare delivered higher-quality responses in an average of 23 seconds, compared with nearly three minutes for the same model using general-purpose web search even when using its highest reasoning setting.

For developers deploying these tools at scale, cost per response matters too. In a separate comparison on the same benchmark, Astra with the healthcare configuration scored higher than Claude Fable 5.2 with web search at less than half the API cost per scored response ($1.37 vs. $2.90), with both systems at their highest reasoning settings.
