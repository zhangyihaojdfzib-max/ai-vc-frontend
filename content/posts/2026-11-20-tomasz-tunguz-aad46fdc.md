In short :Anthropic & SpaceX trade at an identical 9.5x 2027 est. revenue despite 288% & 466% projected growth. Above $1t of enterprise value, public markets stop paying for top-line growth & anchor to a tight 20x to 26x earnings band.

The two largest IPOs in history look like twins.

Anthropic & SpaceX trade within 15% of each other’s market cap, generate within 10% of the same trailing revenue, & are valued on an identical forward multiple of 9.5x.12

Curiously, SpaceX is projected to grow 46% faster than Anthropic, yet the market pays the same 9.5x multiple for both.

![Anthropic & SpaceX forward multiple vs growth scatter with 24 mega caps](/images/posts/ff460a711c9d.jpg)

Across 24 mega-cap software & infrastructure names, both trade in the middle of the pack with roughly ten times the median growth.2

Even though both are growing faster than any software company in history, public markets are not valuing them on forward revenue multiples. They are valued like companies growing 20%, not 200%+.

Expand the lens to the trillion-dollar hyperscalers, & the ceiling is unmistakable : above $1t of enterprise value, no company trades much above 10x forward sales, regardless of whether it grows 16% or 466%.4

P/E is the real anchor. Every trillion-dollar hyperscaler trades in a tight 20x to 26x earnings band. At Microsoft’s 41% net margin, 26x earnings caps forward revenue near 10x ; at Meta & Google’s margins, 20x earnings caps it near 5x.4

![Trailing P/E multiples for trillion-dollar hyperscalers vs sub-$500b software](/images/posts/8f64bfc6547e.jpg)

Public markets are not debating whether Anthropic or SpaceX is the better business : at trillion-dollar scale, multiple expansion is over, & the only way either stock compounds is by growing into the earnings.
