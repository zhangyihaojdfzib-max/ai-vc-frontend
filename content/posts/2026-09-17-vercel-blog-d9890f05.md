GPT-Live 1 from OpenAIis now available on AI Gateway.

GPT-Live 1 is a full-duplex voice model and can listen and speak at the same time. Many voice models use turn detection to respond. Full duplex removes that boundary, so a user can pause, interrupt, or add detail while GPT-Live is speaking.

GPT-Live 1 also supports client delegation. Client delegation lets you choose the background model independently from GPT-Live 1. A basic session runs only the voice model. When deeper work is needed, your application can handledelegation-createdwithany text model on AI Gatewaywhile the conversation continues.
