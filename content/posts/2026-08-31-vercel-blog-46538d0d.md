Vercel now calculates charges forSandbox snapshot storagefrom each day’s average usage and adds them to your monthly invoice. Previously, billing used one average across the entire monthly billing period.

You can track these daily charges on the team’sUsage pageand see how changes in storage affect your cost.

The rate remains $0.08 per GB-month; only how usage is calculated has changed. The update takes effect automatically at the start of the next billing period for Pro and Enterprise teams. No action is required.

Learn more aboutSandbox snapshot storagein the documentation.
