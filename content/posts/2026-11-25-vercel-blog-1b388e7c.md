Workflow SDK5.8 runs workflows on a new execution engine, rebuilt for performance. Step-to-step latency is 12x lower, and large fan-outs, like running 256 steps in parallel, use 14x less function time outside your step code. This directly improves turn times fordurable AI agents, among many other improvements.

To get the new engine, upgrade toworkflow@5.8.0or later. New runs will use the new engine by default, and runs already started on an older version will safely keep running on that version. Make sure your steps areidempotent where possibleto get the most out of this upgrade.

### Copy link to headingDrastically reduced overhead

In ourbenchmarkscomparing5.2.0to5.8.0, p75 step-to-step overhead fell from 85 ms to 7 ms. The median run, including 102 seconds of step work, finished in 111 seconds instead of 3.0 minutes. Running 256 parallel steps in a fan-out added 5.5× less overhead, 2.5 seconds instead of 13.5 seconds, and the median run spent 14× less time in functions beyond the steps' own work, 105 seconds instead of 1,446. A five-turn agent loop that streams its tokens through durable streams and calls tools added 0.9 seconds of overhead instead of 3.0 seconds, 3.3× less. Consult thebenchmarks pagefor how we measure these numbers.

Resuming after 1 second idle time went from 425 ms to 184 ms, 2.3× faster. Resumptions more than 5 seconds out might see slightly larger overhead. You can choose tokeep functions warm for longerto optimize time-to-resume at the cost of additional compute time.

### Copy link to headingSpeculative execution for retryable steps

Many of the above benefits rely on speculative execution of Steps. Only Steps that are idempotent can be run in this way. A step that passes itsstepIdas anidempotency key, like the Stripe charge below, is already safe and needs no changes. If your step is sensitive and can't be retried, mark it withmaxRetries = 0to opt out and ensure it still runs at most once.

You should already be making Steps idempotent wherever possible, or flag them withmaxRetries = 0, to ensure Workflow SDK doesn't re-run your Steps when it's not safe to do so.

## Copy link to headingGet started

Upgrade toworkflow@5.8.0. To keep creating runs on the previous engine after upgrading, setWORKFLOW_SPEC_VERSION=8. If you're on 4.x, start with theupgrade guide, since v5 is a major release.

Using a third-party World?AllWorlds maintained by Vercelalready support the new engine. A third-party World needs tosupport World spec version 9before upgrading gives you any of these improvements. Until it does, new runs keep using the previous engine.
