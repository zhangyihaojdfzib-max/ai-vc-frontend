What are the implications for the AI economy?

Nvidia fused the startup & chip ecosystems in a way we have never seen.Under Huang, Nvidia acted simultaneously as chip supplier, lead venture investor, anchor cloud customer & lease guarantor across more than 200 startups. A sudden transition at the top introduces real uncertainty into the market & especially in startups.
